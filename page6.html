The Amazing Spider-Man

About

Spider-Man is Peter Parker, a teenager who gained superpowers after being bitten by a radioactive spider. Created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko in 1962.

Powers

• Super strength & agility
• Wall-crawling ability
• Spider-sense (danger detection)
• Web-shooting

Villains

Green Goblin, Doctor Octopus, Venom, Sandman, and Electro.

Legacy

"With great power comes great responsibility." One of Marvel's most beloved heroes!
