Nutrition Month 2026

"Healthy Food, Healthy Life"

What is Nutrition Month?

Nutrition Month is celebrated every July in the Philippines. It encourages everyone to eat healthy foods, practice good nutrition, and live a healthy lifestyle.

Why is Good Nutrition Important?

Good nutrition helps us stay healthy, grow stronger, prevent diseases, and have enough energy for school, work, and daily activities.

Healthy Habits

Healthy Habit
Eat fruits and vegetables every day.
Drink plenty of water.
Exercise regularly.
Limit sugary and junk foods.
Get enough sleep every night.

Message

Eating healthy foods and staying active help us live happier and healthier lives. Make healthy choices every day!
